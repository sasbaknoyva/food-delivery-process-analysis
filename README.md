# Food Delivery: Order Process Analysis

Business analysis case study for an online food delivery platform.
The goal is to identify why deliveries are late and orders are cancelled, and to propose process improvements.

> All data in this project is synthetic and created for demonstration purposes.

## Problem
Delivery times are increasing, cancellation rate is growing and customer complaints are rising during peak hours.

## Approach
1. Define the business problem, goals and stakeholders
2. Gather and document requirements (user stories, acceptance criteria)
3. Model the order process (AS-IS and TO-BE)
4. Analyze order data in Excel (KPIs and bottlenecks)
5. Recommend improvements

## Result
- Peak hours drive the problem: delivery time 49.3 min vs 34.8 min off-peak, cancellation rate 15.7% vs 7.2%
- Confirmation, courier assignment and restaurant waiting explain 61% of the peak delay
- 91% of cancellations are caused by process bottlenecks
- Six recommendations in three phases; estimated effect: delivery time about 37 min (from 40.9) and cancellation rate about 6% (from 11%)
