# Business Case

## 1. Background
FoodGo (fictional company) is an online food delivery platform operating in Baku.
Over the last 6 months the number of orders has grown, but service quality has dropped.

## 2. Problem Statement
- Average delivery time increased from 32 to 41 minutes
- Order cancellation rate grew from 6% to 11%
- Most complaints are related to late delivery and wrong or missing items
- Problems are strongest at peak hours (12:00-14:00 and 19:00-22:00)

## 3. Business Goals (SMART)
| # | Goal | Target | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | Reduce average delivery time | from 41 to 34 min | 6 months |
| G2 | Reduce cancellation rate | from 11% to 7% | 6 months |
| G3 | Increase customer rating | from 4.1 to 4.4 | 6 months |

## 4. Scope
**In scope:** order placement, restaurant confirmation, courier assignment, delivery, order status notifications.
**Out of scope:** payment provider integration, marketing campaigns, loyalty program.

## 5. Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Role | Interest |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Orders food | Fast, accurate delivery |
| Courier | Delivers orders | Fair routes, clear instructions |
| Restaurant | Prepares orders | Fewer cancellations, clear order flow |
| Operations Manager | Controls delivery quality | Meeting KPIs |
| Product Owner | Owns the platform | Customer retention and growth |
| Support Team | Handles complaints | Fewer complaints |

## 6. Assumptions and Constraints
- All figures are synthetic and used for demonstration
- Existing mobile apps will be improved, not rebuilt
- No budget for new hardware
